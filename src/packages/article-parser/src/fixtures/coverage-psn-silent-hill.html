    Welcome to the Collectibles & Missables Companion Guide for Silent Hill f. This guide is designed to calmly walk you through when and how to tackle each collectible or missable miscellaneous trophy so your road to the  feels clear and stress-free. While this isn’t a full walkthrough, instead, it’s a structured companion you can follow alongside the main Trophy Guide.

    Silent Hill f follows a young protagonist named Hinako, caught between her community’s suffocating traditions and a spreading, parasitic blight. As reality unravels, you’ll navigate streets, shrines, and households where everyday objects—letters, charms, and votive plaques—carry unbearable meaning. The deeper you venture, the more the world mirrors your fear and the town’s buried sins.

    Unlike typical action-horror, Silent Hill f leans on psychological dread and symbolic storytelling. You’ll find multiple endings, branching conditions, and narrative fragments hidden in documents and ritual spaces. Every choice, detour, and moment of hesitation shapes not only the story’s tone, but also your path toward (or away from) completion.


    This is a complement to the main Trophy Guide (click the image below). If you’re unsure about the recommended route for each playthrough, check the Roadmap in the Trophy Guide first, then follow this Companion Guide for the exact collectible/missable pickups in each area.

    TROPHY GUIDE


    Silent Hill f contains a large number of collectibles and several missable trophies. Many collectibles span multiple playthroughs, so keeping careful track is essential to avoid an extra playthrough. This companion organizes everything so you can obtain all the collectibles in an orderly way without missing any.

    The guide will help you secure the following collectible-related trophies:

    Note on  Omamori Collector & Faith:

    While this companion isn’t a dedicated route for  Omamori Collector, it marks spots where you can find the most valuable Offerings to exchange for Faith. These items appear in the same locations across all three playthroughs, so, as noted in the main Trophy Guide, you can collect and trade them in each run to build up Faith for upgrades. 

    Additionaly, after you pick up Hidden Omamori and Inventory Upgrades, those locations will instead yield Offerings/consumables during a New Game or New Game(+)  playthrough, so use them to keep stacking Faith. For exact thresholds and planning, refer to the main Trophy Guide.

    In addition, this guide will also highlight when to prepare for each missable miscellaneous trophy, so you know exactly where and when to unlock them during your runs.

    Threshold



    100% Trophy Walkthrough: Part 4

    (Video Guide with commentary)


    Map Objectives





    Objective 1

    Shoot the 3 hiss nodes in this junction area to remove the hiss barrier.

    Objective 2

    Press the button on the centre console and complete the training course to reach a object of power, cleanse it to acquire the Shield ability.

     Insular Telekinesis
    Obtain the Shield Ability





    Objective 3

    Take a north turn here and you will see a door with a clearance level 4, go through it.

    Objective 4

    Make your way up this machine area and unlock the door at the top, you will need to use the power cores scattered around to stop the spinning shafts so you can safely cross them.

    Objective 5

    Press the switch to enter the Oceanview Motel.

    Oceanview Motel

    1. Press the reception bell twice to unlock room 224 and head inside. 
    2. Move the radio right, straighten the painting and move the plant pot left.
    3. Now go inside room 226 and grab the key.
    4. Head into the far black prism marked room and press the switch 3 times.

    Objective 6

    Use evade to reach here from the Motel switch or for a safer way, turn around at the switch and take the path on the left. Once you cross the firebreak bridge, head through the large doors.



    Objective 7

    Defeat the enemies and then activate the Atlas Chamber control point and select the following;
    • Abilities.
      • Launch 6.

    Objective 8

    Speak with Arish who is in the office above the control point.

    Objective 9

    Search the container and then kill the enemy so you can access the nearby lab.

    Objective 10

    Grab the Clearance Level 5 card off the desk.

    Objective 11

    Ride the large elevator up.

    Objective 12

    Activate the NSC Power Plant Upper Level control point.

    Objective 13

    Make your way over here and carefully drop down the elevator shaft. Remember evade will nullify fall damage!

    Objective 14

    Activate the Quarry Entrance control point.

    Objective 15

    Place 3 power cores into the slots on the western wall, and then press the button on the centre terminal to detonate the explosives. Collect the Black Rock after from the blast point, then quit to main menu and continue the save to respawn back at the recent control point.




    Objective 16

    Fast travel to the Central Executive control point in the Executive sector and then talk with Marshall who is just by the steps of the board room. After completing the mission, go to the control point and select the following, then once done, use the elevator and head down to the Containment sector.
    • Abilities.
      • Multi-Launch.
      • Shield 1.

    Polaris



    100% Trophy Walkthrough: Part 8

    (Video Guide with commentary)


    Map Objectives






    Objective 1

    Make your way through the Ashtray Maze, killing enemies along the way now that you are able to pass through it. Eventually, you will exit it at the far east side of the map near the next objective. There is no map for this area, but the path is revealed for you as you pass through, which actually makes it more linear than the name suggests.


    Objective 2

    Activate the Dimensional Research control point.

    Objective 3

    Press the red button in the small room here to open the nearby door.

    Objective 4

    Interact with the northwest console here and replicate the shape pictured by pressing the various commands.
    1. Up.
    2. Up.
    3. Left.
    4. Right.
    5. Right.
    6. Right.

    Objective 5

    Back down in the central chamber, shoot the 4 locks on the north blast door to open it.


    Objective 6 - 12

    Cleanse the siphons (satellites). Note that you don't need to kill the enemies, but they will interrupt the cleansing animation, so if any are nearby, you may need to defeat them. The first 3 can be cleansed in any order. After that, each one will appear after cleansing the one before it. Once all of them are cleansed, make your way to the highest point at Objective 12 to finish the mission.

     Polaris
    Complete Mission 9

    Take Control



    100% Trophy Walkthrough: Part 9

    (Video Guide with commentary)


    Map Objective






    Objective 1

    On the notice board are 3 tasks, pick the right task Scan the Forms.



    Objective 2

    Scan the forms until the task is complete.

    Objective 3

    Now scan the 2nd batch of forms on this 2nd scanner until the mission updates again.

    Objective 4

    Grab the Director's Mail from the chute that appears after completing 2 tasks.

    Objective 5

    Drop off the Director's Mail and then collect the Outgoing Mail.


    Objective 6

    Pick the right task again Scan the forms off the notice board.

    Objective 7

    Scan the forms until the task is complete.

    Objective 8

    Grab the Director's Mail again from the chute that appears after completing 1 task this time.

    Objective 9

    Drop off the Director's Mail and then collect the Outgoing Mail again.

    Objective10

    Grab the Director's Mail from the chute that appears straight away this time.

    Objective 11

    Grab your service weapon from the desk.

    Objective 12

    Press the switch in Darling's Office 3 times to warp to the Oceanview Motel.

    Oceanview Motel

    1. Press the bell.
    2. Enter room 226 and grab the key.
    3. Unlock the room opposite 226 and press the switch inside 3 times.

    Objective 13 (unmapped)

    Make your way through the corrupted shifted room that is unmapped until you reach the Slide Projector and interact with it.


    Objective 14 (unmapped)

    Make your way across the corrupted Astral Plane killing enemies along the way to reveal the next section and you will eventually reach Dylan, remember you can use Assist Mode in the options if you are having trouble. Interact with Dylan to complete the game, triggering the credits. Then, after watching or skipping them, you will respawn back in the Director's Office ready to tackle the DLC!
