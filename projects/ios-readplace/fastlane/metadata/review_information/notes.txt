Readplace is a read-it-later app. Sign-in is required to use it.

GETTING STARTED
- On the first screen tap "Log in" or "Sign up". Authentication opens in the EXTERNAL browser (Safari/Chrome) and returns to the app via a readplace:// deep link. This app-switch is intentional (it reuses an existing web session) — please allow it to complete.
- The demo account below has an active subscription. Sign-up is self-serve, so to test ACCOUNT DELETION please create a throwaway account rather than deleting the demo account.

SAVING ARTICLES
- In Safari (or any app), open a web page, tap Share, and choose Readplace. The page is captured and appears in the reading list. Pull to refresh.

NOTES FOR REVIEW
- The app communicates only with https://readplace.com. The NSAllowsArbitraryLoadsInWebContent ATS exception exists solely so the reader and capture web views can load arbitrary user-saved article pages (some are http-only).
- Readplace is a subscription service purchased on the web, not via In-App Purchase. The app is fully functional during the free trial and sells no digital content in-app.
- Account deletion: in the Reading List, tap the person icon (Account) in the top-right toolbar — the account page opens in-app. Under "Danger zone", tap "Delete account" and confirm in the dialog. The account, every saved article, and the reading history are deleted immediately and the app returns to the sign-in screen. (The same flow exists on the web at readplace.com/account.)
