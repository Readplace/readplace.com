Your forwarding addresses

Sign any newsletter with one of these addresses (or use them to email links to your inbox) and it lands in your Readplace queue. It'll pull out the links and queue them up for you to read later.

How it works

  1. Create a forwarding address and copy it.
  2. In your email app, forward a newsletter (or set up forwarding) to it.
  3. New issues show up here automatically.

This is new — right now it just gives you the address. Reading forwarded mail in Readplace is coming next.
