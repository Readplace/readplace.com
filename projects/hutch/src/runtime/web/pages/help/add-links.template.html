Add links with Share

  1. Open a link in any app.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Readplace.

Pin Readplace to the share row

iOS buries new apps at the end of the share row. Favourite Readplace once and it moves to the front.

  1. Tap Share, then scroll the app row right and tap More.
  2. Tap Edit.
  3. Tap the + beside Readplace, then Done.

Sharing captures the full page — even on sites that block crawlers.
