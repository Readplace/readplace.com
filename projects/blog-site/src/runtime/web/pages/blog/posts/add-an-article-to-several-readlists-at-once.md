Some saves answer to 2 readlists at once. An interview about AI in hiring belongs in AI and in Work, and a long read on battery factories is as much Energy as Economy. Neither filing is wrong, the article spans both.

The picker used to treat that as 2 jobs. Filing into the first readlist closed the menu and the page settled. The second readlist meant opening the same menu and finding the same rows again, because the route behind the picker took 1 readlist at a time.

When 2 or more readlists can take the article, the picker now shows a checkbox beside each one and a single Add button under them. Tick AI and Work, press Add.

Both filings land in 1 request.

> **The picker asks which readlists the article fits, not which readlist.**

## Tick the boxes where the filing happens

The checkboxes live on both surfaces that file. In the reader, Add to readlist sits in the bar above the article and opens the menu. On [the readlist](/queue?utm_source=blog-add-an-article-to-several-readlists-at-once&utm_medium=internal&utm_content=readlist), each card's menu carries Add to readlist, which opens the Add to a readlist dialog. The 2 offer the same rows and the same lone Add.

Past 10 rows, a filter appears above them and typing narrows the lot. Create a readlist waits at the bottom of both pickers, so a list that doesn't exist yet gets made without leaving the filing.

## 1 click while only 1 readlist can take it

Checkboxes earn their place only when there's a choice.

While a single readlist can take the article, its row files on 1 click, with no box to tick and no Add button to press.

The picker grows its controls with the account, not ahead of it.

## The Undo covers the whole batch

A multi-add answers with 1 toast, "Added to 3 readlists", and its Undo takes the article back out of all 3. A filing that was 1 readlist too generous doesn't need the full rollback. Each readlist the article sits in is a chip at the top of the reader, and each chip comes off on its own.

Adding is all the checkboxes do.

The dialog's own words cover the rest: "It also stays in All, which keeps everything you save." Ticking Work doesn't take the article out of AI, and nothing here takes it out of All.

## Move kept its radio

Move to another readlist still asks for 1 destination, with a radio beside each row. A move answers a different question, out of 1 readlist and into 1 other, and a move that fanned out would be an add under another name.

Not every picker grew checkboxes.

## A form posts it, scripts or not

The menu is a plain form. The assign route reads the readlist field as many times as the form repeats it, so the no-JavaScript fallback, a select that takes several choices, files the same batch. A picker built as a form keeps working where the script is slow, blocked or stripped.

## A side effect of 15 readlists

An account holds up to 15 readlists now, [a newsletter can feed one](/blog/newsletter-articles-land-in-the-readlist-they-belong-to?utm_source=blog-add-an-article-to-several-readlists-at-once&utm_medium=internal&utm_content=post-newsletter-articles-land-in-the-readlist-they-belong-to), and a written purpose files the fitting links into it on arrival. Lists got narrower, and narrow topics overlap more: the battery-factory read was a 1-readlist save back when Energy and Economy were still 1 list.

The checkboxes came from a reader who asked to file 1 article into several readlists in one go, and that sentence is the whole feature.

## The next save that reads like 2 topics

One is probably near the top of the list already, a piece that 2 of the lists could claim. Open it, press Add to readlist, tick every list with a claim, press Add once.

Back in [the readlist](/queue?utm_source=blog-add-an-article-to-several-readlists-at-once&utm_medium=internal&utm_content=readlist-after), the piece now sits in each list it was ticked into.
