Readplace is a read-it-later app. Save articles from anywhere and read them later in a clean, focused reader — without the clutter, popups, and noise of the open web.

SAVE FROM ANY APP
Open a web page in Safari (or any app), tap Share, and choose Readplace. The article is captured and added to your reading list — no copy-paste, no tab hoarding.

A CALM READING LIST
Everything you saved in one place. Tap an article to read it in a distraction-free reader, and get an AI summary so you can decide what is worth your time before you dive in. Swipe to mark things read as you go.

BUILT ON A DECADE OF READING
Readplace grew out of a personal reading system refined over ten years. It does one thing well: help you actually read the things you meant to read.
