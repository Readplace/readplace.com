Readplace is a read-it-later app for iPhone. Save an article from any app, then read it later in a clean, focused reader — without the clutter, popups, and noise of the open web.

SAVE FROM ANY APP
Open a web page in Safari (or any app), tap Share, and choose Readplace. The Readplace Share Extension captures the article and adds it to your reading list — no copy-paste, no tab hoarding. Share a PDF and it is saved the same way.

A CALM READING LIST
Everything you saved, newest first, in one place. Pull to refresh, keep scrolling for more, and swipe a row to mark it read — a read article leaves the list and stays saved.

READ WITHOUT THE NOISE
Tap an article to open it in a distraction-free reader: the text, and an AI summary so you can decide what is worth your time before you dive in. The original page stays one tap away.

YOUR ACCOUNT, IN THE APP
Sign in, sign out, and delete your account and everything in it — without leaving Readplace.

BUILT ON A DECADE OF READING
Readplace grew out of a personal reading system refined over ten years. It does one thing well: help you actually read the things you meant to read.

Readplace is a web app too: readplace.com opens the same account and the same reading list in any browser, with the fuller set of tools — and what you save on your iPhone is already waiting there.
