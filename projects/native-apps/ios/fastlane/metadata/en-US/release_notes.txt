Listen to your audio and video with a transcript beside it. Upload an MP3 or M4A file, or an MP4 video of up to 2 GB, at readplace.com/upload, then open it in the app to hear it and read along with timestamps. Audio and video share 20 minutes of transcript a day.

When Readplace no longer recognises your sign-in, the app now takes you back to sign in instead of failing quietly every time you open it. A private upload now explains that it has no public link to share, and long books that used to time out now open in the reader and download as markdown.
