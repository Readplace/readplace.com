Readplace is a read-it-later app; sign-in is required.

GETTING STARTED
- Tap "Login" or "Sign up". Sign-in runs INSIDE the app in an ASWebAuthenticationSession sheet, never in a browser. Tap Continue on the "Readplace wants to use readplace.com to Sign In" alert.
- The demo account has an active subscription. Sign-up is self-serve: test ACCOUNT DELETION on a throwaway account, not the demo.

SAVING ARTICLES
- In any app (e.g. Safari) open a page, tap Share, choose Readplace; it appears in the reading list (pull to refresh).
- On the first share, choose any extra readlists and tap "Done". A share is saved to All and to Hand Picked; each readlist's "Shared articles drop here" checkbox sets the extra destinations.

CHANGES IN 1.0.159
- Hand Picked (server-side readlist, no tap needed): the app opens it at launch and every share is added to it. Its header links "Open All on the web", which opens readplace.com in the browser, where "Add to readlist" adds an older save. Cards also show an article's source and how to recover a removed link; offline lists flag downloads with no text.
- Age signal (Declared Age Range, iOS 26.2+ only): on launch the app asks the system for the user's age range with gates 13, 16 and 18; the system shows its own sheet; the user may decline. Under 13, the app shows "Readplace is for people 13 and over." in place of sign-in; if signed in, it first reports the range to readplace.com, which locks the account, and the device is signed out. Age data is never stored on the device or logged. The demo account is an adult; to see the refusal, use a sandbox child account.
- Free plan: an account whose trial or subscription ends is no longer read-only: it keeps every article and saves up to 28 new ones a day. Over the limit the share extension says so and queued uploads retry later.
- Download unread: later pages load with Sec-Purpose: prefetch when the server's next link asks; the server sets how many pages resolve.
- Added on the server since 1.0.151 (no new binary). In the in-app reader: a highlight appears the moment Highlight is pressed; tap one to manage it in place, with its note and a delete-note button; the Highlight bar docks to the bottom on touch screens; highlights follow edits to their passage or are listed as "Not found in this version"; Delete now reads "Delete from this readlist"; marking read or unread shows a one-time "Read status is shared across readlists" notice. Also: add an article to several readlists at once (up to 15), share the original link, report an article, and images keep their space while loading.

NOTES FOR REVIEW
- API traffic goes only to https://readplace.com. The reader and page-capture web views also load saved pages from their sites; NSAllowsArbitraryLoadsInWebContent is only for them, as some are http-only.
- The reader, account and help screens are in-app web views of readplace.com, so they change between submissions.
- Readplace is a web-purchased subscription, not IAP; the app works fully during the free trial, keeps working on the free plan afterwards within daily limits, and sells no digital content in-app.
- TRACKING: the app does not track. No third-party SDK, no AppTrackingTransparency, IDFA or AdSupport reference, and no data shared with third parties or brokers. Its privacy manifest sets NSPrivacyTracking = false with empty NSPrivacyTrackingDomains; the email, user ID and reading history it collects only run the user's own reading list on our server.
- ACCOUNT DELETION: in the Reading List tap "Account" (person icon, top-right); the account page opens in-app. Under "Danger zone" tap "Delete account". A dialog asks for the exact phrase
      delete my account permanently
  Type it and tap "Delete account" (on iOS 16 the field and button appear under the row). The account, saved articles, highlights and notes, and reading history are deleted and the app returns to sign-in; same flow at readplace.com/account.

Demo account: in the App Review Information fields.
