Readplace is a read-it-later app. Sign-in is required to use it.

GETTING STARTED
- On the first screen tap "Login" or "Sign up". Authentication runs INSIDE the app in an ASWebAuthenticationSession sheet (Apple's in-app auth browser) — the app never switches you to Safari or Chrome. iOS shows a "Readplace wants to use readplace.com to Sign In" alert before the sheet opens; tap Continue. The sheet closes by itself once sign-in completes.
- The demo account below has an active subscription. Sign-up is self-serve, so to test ACCOUNT DELETION please create a throwaway account rather than deleting the demo account.

SAVING ARTICLES
- In Safari (or any app), open a web page, tap Share, and choose Readplace. The page is captured and appears in the reading list. Pull to refresh.
- On the first share, choose any additional readlists and tap "Done". Shared articles always appear in the main readlist. The app remembers the additional destinations; the "Shared articles drop here" checkbox in each readlist changes that choice.

CHANGES IN 1.0.117
- The menu next to the reading-list title switches between readlists. The last viewed readlist is restored on launch; To Read and Read tabs apply to the selected list.
- Account settings offer system, light, and dark appearance.
- In the reader, Next Read asks for confirmation before changing articles. EPUB downloads open the iOS share sheet so the file can be saved or opened in another app.
- Page capture and session refresh are more reliable. The sign-in animation is updated, and launching the app preserves audio playing in other apps.

NOTES FOR REVIEW
- The app communicates only with https://readplace.com. The NSAllowsArbitraryLoadsInWebContent ATS exception exists solely so the reader and capture web views can load arbitrary user-saved article pages (some are http-only).
- Readplace is a subscription service purchased on the web, not via In-App Purchase. The app is fully functional during the free trial and sells no digital content in-app.
- TRACKING: the app does not track. It links no third-party SDK of any kind, contains no AppTrackingTransparency, IDFA or AdSupport reference, and shares no data with any third party or data broker. The bundled privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false with an empty NSPrivacyTrackingDomains; the email address, user ID and reading history it collects are used only to run the user's own reading list, on our own server.
- ACCOUNT DELETION: in the Reading List, tap "Account" (person icon, top-right toolbar) — the account page opens in-app. Scroll to "Danger zone". Deletion is guarded by a typed confirmation rather than a dialog: type the exact phrase
      delete my account permanently
  into the text field, then tap "Delete account". The account, every saved article and the reading history are deleted, and the app returns to the sign-in screen immediately. (The same flow exists on the web at readplace.com/account.)
