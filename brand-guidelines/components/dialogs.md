### Dialogs

> **Component:** the shared confirm panel in `@packages/web-shell` (`renderConfirmPopover`, `CONFIRM_POPOVER_STYLES`). The values below belong in that module, never in a page stylesheet.

A dialog is the shared confirm panel, opened as a native popover, and every trigger keeps a plain-form fallback where popover is unsupported. It opens with focus on its commit control (a destructive one included) or its field, never on the panel; the panel keeps `tabindex="-1"` only so a click inside keeps focus inside. The panel is up to 600px wide, `--card` on a 1px `--border` with no shadow, 16px `--radius-lg` corners, over a [scrim and blur](../iconography/shadows-and-elevation.md#shadows--elevation); its padding grows 24px → 32px at 768px.

- **Title** is sans `--text-lg` (18px)/600 on a 28px line in `--foreground`, balanced and 4px above the body. A dialog that first asks a question is titled with the question; one that edits a setting already made is titled with the task ("Edit readlist purpose") and asks the question as a subheading at the title's size and weight, 18px under the title's line box and 4px above the body. The `x` close control is opt-in for dialogs designed with one and for interim dialogs awaiting their own dismiss action.
- **Body** is `--text-sm` (14px)/22px in `--muted-foreground`, with 24px below it. A list of what the action will touch is a bordered box (1px `--border`, `--radius`) of 56px rows with 24px readlist icons and hairline dividers.
- **Illustration.** A confirmation of a consequential action may carry a 64px-tall [illustration](../iconography/illustrations.md#illustrations) at the top padding edge, centred 20px above the title line box (about 24px above its glyphs). Its body is centred and capped at 408px. A naming dialog (create or rename a readlist) has none and no ✕, and is left-aligned: the label, then the field (placeholder "Enter readlist name"), then the inline error, then Cancel and a commit named for the action ("Create readlist", "Save"). A value the server refuses in a dialog field (a readlist's name or purpose) is an inline field error; the field keeps what the reader typed and takes focus back. The readlist cap and a readlist that no longer exists are page alerts, because no name fixes them; at the cap the rail's create row opens the cap alert directly, with no naming dialog. The reader has no page alert, so its create dialog shows the cap under the field. An illustrated confirm that first asks for a choice keeps its art, title and body centred, sets the field left-aligned below the body, and right-aligns the buttons under it (moving or deleting a readlist's articles).
- **Buttons** follow [Pairing](../iconography/buttons.md#pairing): the dismiss comes first, the commit last.
- **Typed confirmation.** A dialog that commits with a typed confirmation shows the label above the field, then Cancel (`neutral`) and the commit. The browser refuses a wrong phrase; a refusal that reaches the server lands on the page's inline form, so the dialog shows no error line.
- **Choice.** A dialog that asks for one of the reader's readlists (moving or adding an article) lists 56px rows with the rail's anatomy — the kind glyph, the name and a trailing radio — neither boxed nor divided, unlike the box that lists what a confirm will touch; **Plan choice** is the named exception. Nothing starts checked unless a no-choice path exists, and the group is `required`. The first radio takes opening focus. The checked row takes the rail's selected tint while the radio's dot carries the state without colour; focus rings the whole row 2px outside it and the radio keeps its own ring, because the radio's ring on the tint is under 3:1. An optional create row closes the list with no divider, unlike the Gmail pickers in [Menus](menus.md#menus), and Cancel and the commit trail on the right.
- **Plan choice.** The plans are one radio group of bordered rows 12px apart. Each row has 16px padding, `--radius-md` corners and a `--card` fill that hovers to `--neutral-hover`, and holds the radio, the plan name (`--text-sm`/600) over its billed line (`--text-sm` in `--muted-foreground`), and the monthly figure on the right. One commit trails **Cancel**. Only the option a no-choice charge would use starts checked: a returning reader's previous plan, otherwise `DEFAULT_BILLING_PLAN`; the featured option is marked by a [tab badge](chips-tags-and-badges.md#chips-tags-and-badges) and a `--primary` border, and being featured never checks it. The figure drops under the text when the plan form is 348px wide or narrower (phones up to 430px in the dialog). The commit takes opening focus. The same form renders inline on `/account/plans`, with no Cancel and named by the page heading; nothing takes focus there.
- **Advisory.** The save tip is illustrated and centred, with no `x` close control; Esc and the backdrop close it. A Tertiary continue control comes before the Primary install action. When the reader already has a capture client, the continue control stands alone as the Primary. The continue control takes opening focus.
- **Selection form.** A dialog that hosts a page's selection form (adding or editing Gmail newsletters) is titled with the task and keeps the ✕. It scrolls as one panel: its checklist and pickers have no scroller of their own, and a picker's list opens in flow under its trigger rather than floating over the form. Cancel and Save sit in a footer row along the panel's bottom edge, on `--card` above a 1px `--border` rule, which stays in view while the form scrolls. Save is Primary inside the dialog even while the page shows another amber CTA, because the dialog's scrim leaves it the one decision on screen.
- **Explanation.** A dialog that explains why a control is off is titled with what is off and gives the reason in the body, with one neutral Close that takes opening focus and no ✕. A private newsletter issue's Share and Download open "Sharing and EPUB download are off": "This issue is an email sent to you, so only you can open it. It has no public link to share or download."
- **Share.** The reader's Share dialog holds the one signed block inside a dialog: the founder row, a 52px portrait beside "Hi, I'm Fayner Brack." and "Thanks for using Readplace.". The link is one copyable box, and five labelled logo circles follow it, then Cancel. There is no ✕, and the dialog never opens by itself. It opens on Copy once the clipboard is writable; otherwise the trigger keeps focus.
