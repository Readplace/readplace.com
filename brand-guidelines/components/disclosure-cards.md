### Disclosure cards

> **Source of truth:** `DISCLOSURE_CARD_STYLES` in hutch's `shared/disclosure-card`. A consumer adds its own block class for layout and never repaints the card, header, title or chevron.

A card that collapses is a `<details class="disclosure-card">`: its `<summary>` is the header and the element after it is the body, so it opens and closes with no script. The reader uses it for the table of contents, Article history and past reads. On desktop all three sit in the rail. On phones past reads sits inside the article card, right under the summary, so it comes before the article; its rail placement is desktop-only. The import page uses it for How it works and What you can import, and its FAQ takes the static header.

- **Card.** `--card` on a 1px `--border`, `--radius-lg` corners and no shadow. It never sets `overflow: hidden`, so a focus ring inside it is not clipped.
- **Header.** 64px tall for a one-line title, with a 20px inset: the title (16px/600 on a 24px line, `--foreground`, wrapping) and a 24px `chevron-down` in `--foreground` that rotates 180° when the card is open. The header hovers to `--muted`, and its focus ring sits 2px outside it.
- **Body.** A full-width 1px `--border` divider separates it from the header. The card sets nothing else inside the body; each consumer lays out its own.
- **Open state is never persisted.** Each consumer picks its default and a reload returns to it: the table of contents and Article history start open, past reads start closed, and the import page's two cards start open while its FAQ rows start closed.
- **Static header.** A card that never collapses (the import FAQ) is a `section.disclosure-card` whose header is a `div` with `.disclosure-card__header--static`. It keeps the 64px header and the title, and has no chevron, no hover fill and no pointer cursor.
- **FAQ rows.** Each question in the static card's body is an independent `<details>`, closed by default and split from the next by a full-width hairline. The whole `<summary>` is the target, with 24px padding, a 16px/500 `--foreground` question, a 24px chevron that turns 180°, and the 2px `--ring` focus ring inset by 2px. The last row, while closed, takes the card's inner bottom corners, so its ring follows the card's edge. An open question keeps the 4px above its answer inside the `<summary>`, so the ring clears the text. The answer (14px/400 `--muted-foreground` on a 22px line) stays in the server-rendered HTML, so the FAQPage JSON-LD matches the page.
- **Compact rows.** Rows inside the past-reads disclosure card, in the rail or the article card, are tighter than a [list](lists.md#lists) row: a 14px/600 title (`--muted-foreground` for an item already read), `16px 20px` padding, 8px between parts and 16px fact glyphs, with a full-width hairline between rows.
- **Table of contents.** The rail's first card lists the article's h2 and h3 headings in document order, as fragment links that are never UTM-tagged. Each h3 nests in its own list under the h2 before it, indented 16px (its row pads `14px 16px 14px 32px`), and every level stays expanded; an h3 with no h2 above it, or whose h2 is left out, stays at the top level. A row reads the heading's text with permalink glyphs (`#`, `¶`, `§`, `🔗`), zero-width characters and line breaks cleaned out; a heading left with no text gets no row. A first heading that repeats the article's title, with only whitespace before it, is left out, because the header above already shows it; a later or introduced match, a lone real section and repeated headings all keep their rows. A row is 14px/500 `--foreground`, padded `14px 16px` on a 21px line (49px), with no hairline; it hovers to `--muted` and draws its focus ring inside itself. The current row (`aria-current="location"`) takes a `--secondary` fill and a 2px inset `--color-brand` edge, with no text shift, and its focus ring sits 2px outside it, because inside it would fall under 3:1 on the light tint. The edge itself is 2.95:1 on its own light tint, an accepted exception: `aria-current` also marks the row, and the edge is 3.62:1 against the rows around it. The card is hidden until the reader view is ready, and below 1024px. The design draws its empty state beside a pending or failed reader, but until the reader is ready there is no article whose sections it could rule out. Its section ids are minted between two sanitiser passes, so the sanitiser has the last word on the article's markup.
