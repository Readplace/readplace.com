### Empty States

An empty list keeps its card, but the card's header (count and sort) is hidden, so the empty state is the card's only content — centred, 48px above and below and 24px at the sides. It holds a spot [illustration](../iconography/illustrations.md#illustrations) (80×64, its drawn size) 24px above a sans title (18px/600, `--foreground`, `text-wrap: balance`), one or two sentences of body 8px under the title (14px on a 22px line, `--muted-foreground`) capped at ~56ch (the one place prose may take a narrower measure than its column), then at most one action 16px below. Pagination is hidden — never "Showing 0 of 0". Distinguish the reasons a list is empty (never used, all caught up, nothing in this tab, nothing in this scope, nothing matching a search or filter), each with its own title and a body naming what to do next. "No matching articles" names what found nothing and offers one action that clears it: **Clear search**, **Clear filters** or **Clear search and filters**. When filters are part of the reason, the body names each ticked chip at 600 in `--foreground` and says the filters stay on until cleared, because a remembered filter is otherwise easy to forget. A setting that isn't set yet (a readlist's purpose) uses the same card, with one neutral button that opens its setup dialog.

The reader's failed-crawl notice uses the same composition in a card of its own: 48px above and below and 42px at the sides (20px on phones), a body capped at 41rem, and one `primary` action, because it is the reader's only way to the article. Where a client could still capture the page and none is installed, an info [alert](alerts-and-status.md#alerts-and-status) closes the card 24px below the action. Its title is "Your link is saved", or "Link saved, but the article is unavailable" when the page is gone.

A rail card's empty state is compact: the table of contents' "No section links" (over "A table of contents isn’t available for this article.") centres a 16px/600 title over a 14px/400 `--foreground` description on a 24px line, 8px apart, in a body at least 240px tall, with no illustration and no action, because an article without section links leaves nothing to do. The wording stays neutral: it says the card has nothing to link to, not that the article has no structure.
