### Menus

> Defined in `src/packages/web-shell/src/shared/menu/menu.styles.ts`.

An overflow menu is a `<details>`/`<summary>` kebab: `ellipsis` on a content row or `ellipsis-vertical` in a narrow rail row. Its 24px `--foreground` glyph is always visible inside a ≥36px hit box, with no fill at rest, on hover or while open. The focus ring sits 2px outside the trigger.

The action panel sits flush below the trigger's hit box with trailing edges aligned. It has a `--card` fill, 1px `--border` edge, `--radius-md` corners, `--shadow-menu`, no inner padding, and a 120px minimum width that grows to fit its content. Full-bleed 44px rows are separated by 1px `--border` hairlines. Each row has 12px side padding, a 20px `--foreground` icon, a 12px gap, and a 14px/500 `--foreground` label. Hover and active fill the whole row with `--muted`; focus draws an inset 2px `--ring`. Delete looks and hovers like every other item; the [dialog](dialogs.md#dialogs) it opens handles the commit. A content row's menu lists its filing action first — "Move to readlist" on a custom readlist, "Add to readlist" on All, drawn with `folder-input` — and Delete last. The reader's menu is the exception to the kebab: its toggle is a neutral icon-only L button on the toolbar row, and its panel hangs 4px below it. A settings card (a readlist's purpose) uses the same kebab and panel, with Edit then Delete. A highlight's actions panel, opened from the highlight in the article, uses the same panel and rows, sits clear of the passage above or below it, and may carry one note block above its rows: 12px side padding, the note's own type, scrolling inside itself so the rows stay visible. Its Add note and Edit note open the note editor, a floating layer with the same `--card` fill, 1px `--border`, `--radius-md` and `--shadow-menu`, 16px padding and up to 26rem wide, with no scrim. It sits clear of the passage, above it or else below, and follows it while the page scrolls; when neither side fits, or the passage is off screen or not found in this version, it docks centred at the bottom of the visual viewport. It picks above, below or docked again each time the page scrolls, whereas the highlight's actions panel keeps its first pick until it is asked to pick again; the two differ on purpose, so do not align them. It shows its title, then the passage quoted in `--muted-foreground` and clamped to three lines, then the note field and its row of [S buttons](../iconography/buttons.md#pairing). The toolbar's Add note opens the same editor beside the selection.

The desktop header account dropdown uses the same panel and row values without the shared classes. Gmail sender and destination pickers and the reader's Add to readlist picker are selection lists: they use the panel tokens with a 4px inset, undivided 8px-radius options and a divider above the create row. The reader's picker hangs from the trigger's leading edge, 4px below it, at least the trigger's width and at most 20rem wide. Its 44px rows hold 20px `--foreground` folders and 14/400 labels, and one full-bleed hairline sits before its muted "Create a readlist" row, which opens the naming dialog.

A menu moves no focus when it opens; focus stays on its toggle. A highlight's actions panel is a dialog and opens on its first action, as the reader's picker does. The reader's picker and the Gmail pickers open on their first option: the Gmail readlist picker on its new-name box while a readlist can still be created, and on its first tickable readlist once capped.
