### Settings pages

A settings page (the account page) is one column of headed sections at an 880px measure inside the frame (see [App Pages](../spacing-and-layout.md#app-pages)), opened by a 24px/700 `h1` and a 16px/500 `--muted-foreground` lede.

- **Sections.** A section's sentence-case heading (18px/700, `--foreground`) sits outside its card, 12px above it, and sections stack 24px apart.
- **Cards of rows.** A settings card is `--card` with a 1px `--border` and `--radius-lg`, carries no padding of its own, and splits its rows with full-width 1px `--border` hairlines. It is not a content [list](lists.md#lists).
- **Row anatomy.** A row pads 24px all round: a 48px `--muted` icon well holding a 20px `--foreground` glyph, 16px to the text (a 16px/600 title on a 22px line over a 16px/400 `--muted-foreground` description, 2px apart), and the actions 32px after the text, centred on the row.
- **The Profile row** comes first. Its leading slot is the identity disc instead of an icon well: 48px `--color-avatar` with white 18px/700 initials, `aria-hidden`. The display name is the title over the email, or the email alone is the title. Edit (`neutral`, `pencil`) opens "Edit profile", a dialog with one Name field; a blank Save clears the name. A native app surface shows the row without Edit.
- **The Security row** comes second: a `lock` well, "Password", "Last updated {relative}." (left off when unknown) and a `neutral` M "Change password" (`pencil`) that opens the Change password page. An account with no password has no Security section.
- **Actions** are M buttons that hug their labels on the trailing edge, 8px apart. Below 768px they drop under the text at the leading edge, and a choice row's options share the width.
- **A choice row** is one `neutral` button per option in a `role="group"` named by the row title, and one press saves. The pressed option carries the radio dot (`.form-choice--mark`) and no tint: the dot is the state.
- **A destructive row** ends in a red `destructive` trigger that opens a confirm [dialog](dialogs.md#dialogs) holding the typed confirmation. Where popover is unsupported, or after the server refuses the phrase, the same form renders under the row with a `destructive` submit.
- **A remembered-choice row** (Your choices on a readlist's Preferences tab) ends in one `neutral` M Remove that posts at once, with no confirm: Remove deletes only the remembered choice and leaves the article where it is.
- **Connected apps** follows Subscription & billing, one row per app signed in to Readplace: its logo in the well, its name, what it does or where it connects from, its latest sign-in under that ("Chrome on macOS · Sydney, Australia · Signed in 3 days ago", a part left off when unknown, and no line for an app with no recorded sign-in), and a `destructive` M "Disconnect". Its dialog, "Disconnect {app}?", asks without a typed phrase. Where popover is unsupported, a `<details>` holds the same words and a `destructive` submit. With nothing connected, the card keeps one row of the same anatomy (the `plug` well, "No apps connected yet", how it fills, and a `neutral` M "See ways to save"), not the centred empty-list card.
- **The billing card.** Subscription & billing is one settings card of up to three sections, split by full-width hairlines. The plan summary is a 16px/600 title with its status chip 8px after it, then the price and the next payment in 16px/400 `--muted-foreground`, 16px apart, and the state's one action on the trailing edge, centred on the title and price. The payment method is a 72×48 brand tile, the card title with its "Primary" badge, and "Expires MM/YY". Billing history is a settings row. Cancelling and card changes open in an in-place panel under their section (`?manage=`) whose opener becomes Done; they are never a second loud button beside the summary.
