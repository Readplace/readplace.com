### Search and filters

The readlist narrows its open tab with a search row and a filter drawer. The search is a GET form, and the drawer posts and lands back on a link that names its filters, so the URL holds what the reader asked for.

- **Search row.** A 48px [field](../spacing-and-layout.md#form-inputs) led by the 20px `search` glyph, then the 48px square `neutral` filter button 8px after it, 32px under the tab strip and 16px above the list. Field and button share one line at every width, as the phone design draws them, so where the field is too narrow its placeholder gives way with `text-overflow: ellipsis` instead of the row stacking. It shows only while the open tab has rows, or while the first save into an empty list is in flight, so that card lands where its [skeleton row](loading-states.md#loading-states) stood.
- **Search is submitted, never live.** Enter runs it; nothing filters while the reader types.
- **Filter drawer.** A native popover on the right edge, `min(600px, 100%)` wide and full height: `--background` with a 1px `--border` left edge and no shadow, over the dialog [scrim and blur](../iconography/shadows-and-elevation.md#shadows--elevation). A 72px header (the drawer title and the `x` close) and a footer (Clear all, then Apply filters) are pinned above and below dividers, and only the body scrolls. It opens with focus on the first chip of the first group. Where popover is unsupported, the same form opens from a `<details>` panel under the button.
- **Filter chips.** Each group (Reading time, Saved date) is a row of [checkbox pills](chips-tags-and-badges.md#chips-tags-and-badges): ticks inside a group widen the list, and ticks across groups narrow it.
- **Active filters.** While any chip is ticked, the filter button swaps its 1px `--border` edge for a 2px `--foreground` edge, the same neutral ink that borders a ticked chip, carries an 8px `--foreground` dot, and is named "Filters (N)". It is never amber: amber marks a selected rail row, not a filter.
- **Filters are remembered; a search is not.** The ticked chips stay on for the signed-in session, across readlists, tabs, paging, sort, reloads and a return from any other page. Clear all, logging out, logging in and saving an article from the readlist clear them. A search lives only in the URL: it survives paging, sort and tab switches, and moving to another readlist or saving an article drops it. A link that names its own filters shows those for that page and leaves the remembered ones as they were.
